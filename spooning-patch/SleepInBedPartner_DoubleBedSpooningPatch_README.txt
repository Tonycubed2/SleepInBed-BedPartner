Sleep In Bed SKSE - Bed Partner: Double Bed Spooning patch 1.0.0, by tonycubed2

For Double Bed Spooning - OAR by CommonDominator (Nexus 118088). All credit for the spooning
animations goes to him; this patch only replaces the 3 OAR config files.

What it does
Double Bed Spooning picks a pose with a separate random roll for each sleeper, so two people in
the same bed often get different poses, and someone alone in a double bed gets one too.
With this patch, Sleep In Bed SKSE - Bed Partner (1.14.0 or newer) picks one pose per night and
gives it to you and your bed partner only. Everyone else sleeps normally.
Options: SKSE Menu > Sleep In Bed > Bed Partner > Spooning.

Requirements
- Sleep In Bed SKSE - Bed Partner 1.14.0 or newer
- Double Bed Spooning - OAR (the OAR version; the DAR version is not supported)
- Open Animation Replacer

Install
Install with your mod manager below Double Bed Spooning - OAR, so these config.json files win.
Enable SleepInBedPartner - Spooning.esp (ESL flagged: does not use a plugin slot).
